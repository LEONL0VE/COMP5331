Earthquake clusters, explained

In 2024, 1,260 earthquakes of magnitude 2 or more shook Southern California. See how DBSCAN groups them, why each one ends up where it does, and how far it would have to move to join another group.

Data from the USGS earthquake catalog. DBSCAN after Ester et al. (1996), explanations after ExDBSCAN by Matthews et al. (2026).
